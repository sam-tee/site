## Hosting (Cloudflare)

This site is served through [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/).
To deliver pages and block abuse, Cloudflare processes your IP address and
basic request details (TLS handshake, caching, bot filtering). The lawful basis
is legitimate interests: keeping the site fast, available, and secure.

Data may be processed outside the UK/EEA, including in the US, under
[Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa)
(EU Standard Contractual Clauses plus Data Privacy Framework certification).
See also the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Everything else

Everything else is local only: no cookies, no analytics, no third-party
requests and the light/dark theme choice lives solely in your browser
